Magical Potions

Polyjuice Potion

Ingredients: Lacewing flies, leeches, powdered Bicorn horn

Effect: Allows the drinker to take the appearance of someone else

Felix Felicis

Ingredients: Ashwinder eggs, squill bulb, Occamy eggshell

Effect: Grants the drinker luck for a period of time

Amortentia

Ingredients: Ashwinder egg, rose thorns, peppermint oil

Effect: Powerful love potion

Draught of Living Death

Ingredients: Valerian root, Sopophorous bean, Peppermint oil

Effect: Induces a deep, nearly permanent sleep

Veritaserum

Ingredients: Hellebore, Flobberworm mucus, Peppermint oil

Effect: Forces the drinker to tell the truth
